```text
"Create a 15-second realistic cinematic slice-of-life video in 16:9, 24fps. Visual-only video, no dialogue, no voice, no music, no sound effects, no subtitles, no text on screen. Warm golden-hour sunlight, soft handheld camera, muted film color grade, slight grain, natural shadows, peaceful nostalgic neighborhood mood. Fully realistic live-action style, not game-like, not animated.

Main character: a young Korean woman in her early 20s with messy dark brown hair tied in a loose ponytail, soft natural face, calm gentle expression, dark gray cropped sleeveless tank top, loose light-wash blue jeans, black necklace, casual sneakers. Keep her look consistent in every shot.

Location: a long narrow real-life residential alley between tall tan stone and concrete walls. Rear tracking shot. The Korean woman walks slowly through the long narrow alley from behind. Golden sunlight stretches across the tan walls. Camera follows with natural handheld motion.

0:02–0:04:

0:04–0:06:
Medium side shot. The cat walks near her feet and looks up. She smiles softly, bends down slowly, and extends one hand in a gentle careful way.

0:08–0:10:
Emotional close-up. The woman smiles warmly while petting the cat. Sunlight flickers through tree leaves across her face, shoulders, and hair. Keep the moment soft, calm, and human.

0:10–0:12:
Medium-wide shot. The cat walks ahead a few steps, then looks back at her. She stands slowly and follows it with a curious peaceful smile.

0:12–0:14:
Front tracking shot. She walks toward camera with the cat beside her near the wall. She looks down at the cat and smiles. The long alley, tan walls, blue wall far behind, trees, and bicycle remain visible.

0:14–0:15:
Final close shot. The cat brushes against her leg. She looks toward the camera with a soft peaceful smile, then looks down at the cat again. Hold the warm golden alley atmosphere for a gentle cinematic ending."
```

### Macro spices collapsing into a cosmos, stop-motion style

<a href="https://x.com/noorwithwifi/status/2051983365688242324"><img src="https://pbs.twimg.com/ext_tw_video_thumb/2051983228416999424/pu/img/fy0ALsrkz6LWf7jm.jpg" width="460" alt="Macro spices collapsing into a cosmos, stop-motion style"></a>

**[NoorAI](https://x.com/noorwithwifi)** · [@noorwithwifi](https://x.com/noorwithwifi) · Seedance 2.0 · 2026-05-06 · 4.3K views · [▶ Watch on X](https://x.com/noorwithwifi/status/2051983365688242324)

<details><summary><b>Prompt</b></summary>

```text
Create a 15-second cinematic, ultra-detailed macro stop-motion style video in vertical 9:16 format.

Scene opens on a rustic wooden table bathed in warm sunrise light. A small pile of mixed desi spices (turmeric, chili flakes, cumin, cardamom) sits still in extreme macro close-up. Dust particles gently float in visible light rays. The atmosphere is quiet, earthy, and intimate.

At 3 seconds, a human finger slowly enters frame and pinches the spice mix. As the spices are lifted, the grains begin to defy gravity and float in slow motion. Each particle subtly starts glowing with warm tones (gold, red, amber).

At 7 seconds, the spices suddenly burst outward in slow motion. Turmeric transforms into glowing golden nebula clouds, chili flakes become fiery red supernova explosions, cumin seeds turn into tiny orbiting planets, and pepper particles dissolve into deep space voids. The camera begins a slow orbital rotation around the expanding structure.

From 11 to 14 seconds, the frame fully transforms into a cosmic universe made entirely of spices. Swirling galaxies form from spice textures, stars sparkle like salt crystals, and nebulae flow like colored powder clouds. The camera slowly pulls back to reveal the full universe scale.

At 14 seconds, the entire cosmic structure collapses smoothly back into the original spice pile. The finger re-enters the frame, resetting everything as if nothing happened, creating a seamless loop.

Use macro cinematography, 100–150mm lens look, shallow depth of field, soft volumetric lighting, and smooth high-frame-rate slow motion. Transition lighting from warm earthy tones in the beginning to deep cosmic blues and purples during transformation. Sound design should include soft kitchen ambience, rising tonal hum, particle crackles, deep bass whooshes during explosion, and a subtle “time reset” tick at the loop point.

Ensure smooth, physically believable particle motion even during surreal transformation. Maintain highly satisfying visual symmetry and perfect loopability.
```

</details>
